What?
The Biovia Project Call 2026.2 has been opened.
In 2026 Biovia – supported by VLAIO – will provide € 8 million for earmarked, non-dilutive funding for collaborative projects addressing unmet needs.
Project Call 2026.2 is the first to align with the newly launched Biovia Roadmaps—
six topics
identified by Biovia members as priorities for health innovation in Flanders, based on their needs and the strengths of our ecosystem.
Guided by these Roadmaps, Biovia will help members build projects, find partners and funding—turning strategy into tangible impact. The six topics are:
Market Acceleration
Value-Based Healthcare
Clinical Trials
TechBio & Precision Health
Immunology
Agtech & Industrial Biotech
Who?
The call is intended for companies developing a promising innovative health solution that needs funding and are looking to collaborate with complementary partners to accelerate development. Consortia must consist of at least three partners, must include Biovia members and may combine expertise from industry, research institutes, hospitals and healthcare organizations.
UHasselt can act as a coordinator or research partner, depending on the grant type chosen.
The following grant types are possible:
- Research project
- Development project
- Feasibility study
- cSBO (only 1 in 2026)
- ICON
- COOCK+
- TETRA
You can always contact Biovia to see within which grant type your proposal fits best.
