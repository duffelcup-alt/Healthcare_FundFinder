The
Baekeland
programme offers the opportunity to perform
PhD research in close cooperation with a company
. It supports basic research that has clear economic objectives and offers added value to the company involved in the project.
Innovation
mandates provide support to
postdoctoral researchers
who wish to actively achieve the effective transfer, the exploitation and the
utilisation
of their research findings, either through a collaboration with an existing company, or through the establishment of a new spin-off company.
The second Baekeland and Innovation mandates calls of 2026 are now open, with
25 August
as the
internal KU Leuven deadline
to provide
LRD
with the budget table, a proof of admission to the doctoral programme, and the link to the VLAIO online application form. The final deadline for the full proposal at VLAIO is
1 September
(noon).
