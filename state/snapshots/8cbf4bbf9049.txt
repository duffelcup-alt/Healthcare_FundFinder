VLAIO Funding
Innovation happens when diverse fields join forces
Biovia provides non-dilutive funding – made available by Flanders Innovation & Entrepreneurship (VLAIO) – to consortia developing disruptive solutions for unmet medical needs.
Consortia must consist of at least three partners from the sectors medical biotechnology, medtech or digital health.
There are two funding calls per year, which we announce on our website, in our
newsletter
and via our social media.

Different needs, different funding options
Biovia offers funding through
multiple VLAIO grants
, depending on the partners and the needs of the project.
For projects only involving one or two partners, members can apply directly to
VLAIO
.
For projects involving a consortia of three or more partners,
members must go through Biovia
to apply for the VLAIO funding using the correct forms and channels.

Feasibility study
Innovative projects exploring tech and market feasibility. Also open to pre-seed start-ups.
Research project
An innovative idea that requires more insight via research to create a new product.
Development grant
Development of new or existing product with existing research.
CSBO
Cluster Strategic Basic Research: Innovative research that offers prospects for economic or societal applications such as a new generation of products, processes or services.
ICON
Highly innovative, interdisciplinary collaborative research between research institutes and industry. Universities/research centers receive 100% funding.
COOCK
Knowledge transfer projects that accelerate the introduction of technology to a broad group of companies through use cases.
TETRA
Accelerating the introduction of technology developed by colleges/universities to enterprises and NGOs.
