"To be prepared is half the victory."
Miguel de Cervantes
A quote by the author of Don Quixote that resonates well with the world of research and innovation. And certainly the world of finance.
Innoviris' mission is to support and finance cutting edge solutions that solve local and global challenges. Which need to be prepared today.
Browse through our schedule of upcoming programmes and initiatives regularly to identify timely funding opportunities that can help you realise your ambitious plans.
This schedule is subject to change. Only when the programme has been formally announced will it start. In case of inconsistencies between this table and the official regulations of the programme, the regulations always take precedence.
