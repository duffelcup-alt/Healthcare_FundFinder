update
From August 12, 2026, this subsidy is temporarily suspended. All applications for this subsidy submitted before August 12, 2026, will be processed and paid in accordance with the applicable rules.
In the life of a company, it is sometimes helpful and necessary to use a specialist consultant to optimise your business. The Brussels Region offers a financial intervention which will cut the cost of the consultant.
What is the purpose of the grant?
Cover a proportion of the costs for consultancy assignments.
Areas covered by this grant
Commercial, strategic, or financial analysis to identify, develop, or enhance revenue sources
Legal study (excluding litigation)
Technical study
Communication strategy
Management and change of business organisational processes
Who is it for?
Very Small Enterprises (VSEs) in Brussels
Some sectors of activity are excluded. We therefore recommend that you always check whether your business falls within the eligible sectors.
There is an alternative for starting entrepreneurs who do not have a company number yet:
the start-up grant
from Brussels Economy and Employment
How the grant works
Reimbursement rate: 25% to 70% based on various criteria. Expenses are partially reimbursed.
Important to note: grant application must be submitted before incurring costs and starting the consultancy assignment!
