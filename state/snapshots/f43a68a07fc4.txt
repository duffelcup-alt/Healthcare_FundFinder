Startup Boost is Wallonie Entreprendre's early-stage investment program for tech startups in Wallonia.
The program provides a structured €100,000 convertible loan investment, enabling founders to accelerate development, validate their market and prepare for subsequent funding rounds.
What is
Startup Boost?
Startup Boost supports very young technology-driven startups with a first structured investment and a clear selection process.
The objective is to help startups demonstrate market traction and build the foundations for scalable growth
Each call acts as a launchpad, combining :
a first structured investment,
a clear and fast selection process,
evaluation by experienced entrepreneurs, industry executives and investors.
Why
apply?
Startup Boost is designed to :
bridge the gap between innovation and first equity rounds,
enable startups to validate technologies and business models,
back innovations creating sustainable competitive advantage,
strengthen priority tech ecosystems in Wallonia.
Who
can apply ?
The program is open to Walloon startups that :
Less than 3 years old
Develop digital
and/or technological innovation
Address a clearly identified market need
Aiming for scalable growth
Led by a team able to execute quickly
Having their operating headquarters in the Walloon Region.

Structured support when needed
If a startup is not yet supported by an incubator or structured program, the investment may be conditional upon setting up coaching or structured support.
After the investment, selected startups benefit from regular follow-up, including quarterly exchanges and ongoing interaction with the Wallonie Entreprendre team.
Part of a broader ecosystem
Startup Boost is embedded in the Walloon entrepreneurial continuum and is complementary to more mature funding instruments.
From application to final decision:
approximately
8 weeks
Each call is reviewed by a Selection Committee composed of experienced entrepreneurs, industry executives and investors. The objective is to challenge applications against real market conditions and identify startups with strong scaling potential.
