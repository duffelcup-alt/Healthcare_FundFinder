The problem

Belgian funding is split across four governments, and it keeps moving.

Research and innovation support is mostly regional, tax measures are federal, and each agency runs its own website, portal and rules. Budgets get cut and calls get suspended mid-year. Founders lose weeks working out what still applies to them.

Kept current

Checked against the official pages every day.

A scraper reads each scheme's official page daily and flags what changed. A person reviews every change before the data is updated, and each scheme shows the date it was last checked.

  1. Daily checkEvery official page and the agencies' overview pages are read and compared with the day before.
  2. Change detectedWording like “suspended”, “geschorst” or “suspendu” and new deadlines are flagged, in Dutch, French and English.
  3. Human reviewNothing reaches the site without a person confirming it on the source page.
  4. You see the dateEvery scheme shows when it was last verified, so you know how fresh the information is.
