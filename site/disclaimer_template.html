In short: this tool helps you find public funding schemes, but it is information only, not advice. It can be incomplete, wrong or out of date. Always check the official page and the granting agency before you apply or make a decision. What you type stays in your browser.

1. What this tool is

The Healthcare Startup Funding Finder (“the tool”) lists federal and regional public funding schemes in Belgium that healthcare startups may be able to use, and suggests which ones fit a profile you enter. It is an independent prototype run by its owner (“we”). It is not an official government service.

2. Not advice

Nothing in the tool is legal, tax, accounting, financial, investment or grant-writing advice. It does not take your full situation into account. For decisions about applying, structuring a project, tax measures or investments, speak to the granting agency or a qualified adviser.

3. Accuracy and how current the data is

  • The data is collected from official sources such as VLAIO, Innoviris, hub.brussels, SPW Économie Emploi Recherche, Wallonie Entreprendre and the federal public services. Every scheme links to the page it was taken from.
  • Each scheme shows the date it was last checked. An automated scraper looks for changes on those pages, but it can miss changes, misread a page or fail to reach it. Changes it finds are reviewed by a person before the data is updated.
  • Budgets, rates, conditions and deadlines can change, be suspended or end at any time, sometimes without notice. A scheme shown as open may already be closed, and the reverse.
  • Where the tool and the official source differ, the official source and the decision of the granting body always prevail.

4. Matches, scores and estimates

  • Matches and fit scores come from simplified rules based on what you enter. A match does not mean you are eligible, and a scheme not shown may still apply to you.
  • Amounts marked “Indicative” are rough calculations. They don't take into account cost eligibility, bonuses, cumulation rules, de minimis ceilings or the agency's assessment. The actual amount, if any, is decided by the granting body.
  • Funding is never guaranteed. Applications are assessed by the granting body, often competitively and subject to budget.
